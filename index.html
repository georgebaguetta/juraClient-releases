Everything a mail app should do, without the wait

Instant inbox

IMAP IDLE delivers new mail the moment it arrives. A local SQLite store makes every folder open at once, online or offline.

Smart tabs

People, updates and newsletters are sorted into tabs, so the mail from real people stays on top.

Codes and flights

Sign-in codes get a copy button. Flight confirmations become a card with airline, route, time and booking code.

Undo Send and Send Later

Take a message back after you press Send, or schedule it for the morning.

Full-text search

FTS5 search goes through every message you have, in milliseconds.

Rules and one-click unsubscribe

Move, mark and archive mail automatically. Leave mailing lists without opening them.

Sender checks

Warnings for look-alike addresses and messages that fail authentication.

Easy setup

Type your address. juraClient finds the servers for you.

Optional AI summary

Get the gist of a long thread with your own API key. Off until you turn it on.

A calendar that speaks CalDAV

Connect iCloud, Fastmail, Nextcloud, Radicale, Baïkal, Zoho or Yahoo. Day, week and month views, repeating events that keep their time when the clocks change, and invitations that go from your inbox straight into the calendar.

Native on both systems

The mail engine is written once in Rust: sync, storage, MIME, SMTP and the calendar. On the Mac it runs under a SwiftUI interface; on Windows under WinUI 3. Each one looks and feels at home.

Passwords live in the macOS Keychain or the Windows Credential Manager, never in plain files.

  • SwiftUI · macOS
  • WinUI 3 · Windows
  • Rust core · IMAP · SMTP · CalDAV · SQLite
